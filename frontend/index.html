    How it works

    1. 1. Connect your wallet Use any wallet that holds your Agent ID, on BNB Chain (chain 56). No account, no sign-up, no seed phrase — ever.
    2. 2. Check in once a day Press “Check In Today” and confirm the transaction in your own wallet. You only pay the BSC network gas fee.
    3. 3. Keep the streak alive Check in every day to grow your streak and unlock a soulbound badge. Miss a day and the streak resets to zero.

    About & FAQ

    What is AgentStreak?

    A simple daily check-in dApp for Agent ID holders. Each check-in is recorded on-chain, building a public streak and a soulbound badge tied to your wallet.

    Who can check in?

    Anyone holding at least one Agent ID (SPACE ID Agent ID, ERC-721) on BNB Chain. The contract checks your balance before accepting a check-in.

    What does it cost?

    Nothing except the BSC network gas fee for the check-in transaction. There is no fee charged by AgentStreak.

    What is the badge?

    A non-transferable (soulbound) badge level that grows with your streak. It cannot be sold or transferred — it only proves your consistency.

    What if I miss a day?

    Your streak resets to zero, but your total check-in count is kept. Start a new streak the next day.

    Does AgentStreak hold my funds or keys?

    No. AgentStreak never asks for your seed phrase or private key, never takes custody of anything, and never asks you to approve token spending. Every transaction is confirmed by you, in your own wallet.

    Free to use — you only pay BSC network gas. No rewards or returns are promised. AgentStreak never asks for your seed phrase or private key.
